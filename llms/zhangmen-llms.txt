# Zhangmen

> Zhangmen (掌门1对1 / 掌门教育) is a Shanghai-based online education company offering premium one-on-one and small-group tutoring to K-12 students across China. The platform pairs students with elite instructors and layers on data-driven learning analysis (diagnostic assessment, personalized study plans, real-time classroom monitoring, automated learning reports, parent progress tracking). It also runs quality-education modules (public speaking, creative art, programming, comprehensive/mathematical thinking) and a piano accompaniment-practice product. Zhangmen is a direct-to-consumer web and mobile app play delivered in Simplified Chinese; as of this writing it exposes no public developer API, SDK, or documentation surface.
